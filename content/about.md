# About

Welcome! I'm **Aymen Boukhari**, a computer science student and AI researcher with interests in internals of large language models and  self superived learning for vision and task 

This website is where I document what I learn, the ideas I explore, and the experiments I run. Rather than serving as a collection of quick notes, it aims to provide thoughtful explanations of research papers, technical concepts, and my own investigations.

## What you'll find here

- In-depth reviews and discussions of machine learning papers
- Experiments and reproducible implementations
- Topics in robotics, self superivsed learning , LLMs ..etc stay tunned for more !
- Insights gained while working on research projects

## Why this blog exists

I believe that understanding modern AI requires more than reading papers—it requires asking questions, reproducing results, and experimenting with ideas.

This blog is my way of sharing that process. Some posts explain established concepts, while others document ongoing explorations and open questions. My hope is that these articles are useful to students, researchers, and anyone interested in how intelligent systems work.
